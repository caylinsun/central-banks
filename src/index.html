Method, limitations and sources

Rates are verified against dated official publications. Refresh checks official current-rate pages and available news feeds; a failed check keeps the last verified value. Leadership biographies, committee stance and interpretation are editorial research dated 27 September 2026.

Commercial-bank prime rates are not central-bank policy rates. Singapore instead manages the S$NEER exchange-rate band. Stance labels summarise recent votes and communication; they are not permanent descriptions of individuals or institutions.
